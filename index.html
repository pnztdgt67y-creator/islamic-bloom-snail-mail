ISLAMIC BLOOM • AUGUST

Come Back to Allah

🌷

Dear beautiful soul,

Maybe you have been feeling distant lately. Maybe your prayers have felt rushed, Qur'an became “tomorrow”, or life simply became so busy that your heart began to miss something.

This month's letter carries one gentle reminder:

Come back.

Not when you become perfect. Not once everything is fixed. Begin now, with one sincere step.

“Do not lose hope in Allah's mercy.”Qur'an 39:53

Make sincere tawbah. Say Astaghfirullah. Open the Qur'an. Make one honest dua. Pray the next salah with your heart present.

Your garden doesn't bloom all at once. Neither do you.

With love,
Islamic Bloom 🌸
